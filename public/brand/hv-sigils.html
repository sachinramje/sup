Recommendation — 01 Converge

The one that's both

Converge is the only mark here that reads as a symbol at a glance and resolves into an H when you look twice. You get the sigil you're drawn to and the letter that rolls up every Hyper brand — without choosing between them.

Axis is the purest and the closest to your reference. Frame is the most editorial — brackets are literally what you put around the part that matters. Stack is the most literal statement of what you do.
